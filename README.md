# Sadie's Clubhouse

A lost 90s shareware activity center, in crappy late-90s 3D. Sadie the cat has decided to share her
clubhouse with all her friends. Walk in through the front door, up the spiral staircase, and through
a door into an activity. Outside the gate there's a little town with more of them. Sadie is in every
one, in a different way each time.

**Play it:** https://yaosio.github.io/sadies-clubhouse/

WASD and the mouse, or a thumb stick on a phone. It saves as you go, in your own browser.

## What's inside

Sadie's Dropper World, TypeFitter Deluxe 3.1, Brickbuster '96, the Music Room, the aquarium (and an
ocean), Cats Only, Space Adventure, the Hedge Maze, Clyde's House, Chooter's Paint Shop and Marbles'
Cut & Curl. Each one's own page in `docs/<name>/README.md` says what it is.

## For the curious

Everything else is in the docs: `docs/TASKS.md` says which page to read for which job, and
`docs/clubhouse/ARCHITECTURE.md` shows how it fits together. `CLAUDE.md` is how it gets built, checked
and published.
