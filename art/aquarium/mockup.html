This is where the ocean starts. In the real thing your view keeps going: it looks up, and you're out at sea with a boat's cockpit round the screen. No loading screen.

OCEAN COMING SOON!

  • Walk up to the tank. Sadie's swimming in it in her diving suit (fishbowl helmet, air tank, flippers), with the fish.
  • Its door, out on the landing (press OUTSIDE THE DOOR, or walk up to the door and press E): sea blue, with a sign that's a little tank of its own with a goldfish in it, a porthole, and water seeping out underneath. Inside, its sign is painted over, like every room's door.
  • The cardboard sign taped to the glass says DON'T TAP ON THE GLASS!! (she means it).
  • Stand close to the glass and look at it: a TAP THE GLASS prompt shows up (E on a computer, a big button on a phone). Swiping to look around never taps it.
  • Tap it: the fish scatter, Sadie turns and glares, then your view rises over the rim and dips into the water.
  • The cabinet on the right wall, OCEAN FINDS, is where things you pick up in the ocean will go. Press SHOW OCEAN FINDS to see it full.

It's silent on purpose. Drag the picture to look around, WASD (or the thumb stick on the phone view) to walk.
