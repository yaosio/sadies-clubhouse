HOW IT PLAYS

  • Every button changes the text. Sadie gushes about it, with a made-up fact about how the computer does it.
  • Her TEXT LOVE meter goes up or down after each change. It looks like she has opinions, but it's a dice roll, rigged to fill somewhere between the 4th and 10th change.
  • When it's full, FIT IT! lights up. The program makes a big show of fitting, fails, and says you win anyway. A certificate prints, then a new sentence arrives.
  • The box is always a little too small. The status bar at the bottom tells you by exactly how many pixels, and the box gets closer as Sadie's meter fills.
