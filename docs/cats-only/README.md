# Cats Only

The clubhouse's eleventh activity (`src/activities/cats-only/`), behind the seventh door on the first
landing, straight above the front door. Like Brickbuster '96 it lives in the clubhouse itself, not on
a computer. The door says CATS ONLY and beside it, out on the landing, a red button says DO NOT
PRESS. Open the door without pressing it and it's a closet of a room with a cat bed in it: room to
step inside and that's all. Press the button and then open the door, and an avalanche of Sadies
pours out, meowing, runs through the clubhouse and out of the front door. Once the front door shuts
behind them they vanish.

## Design pillars (the owner's rules; these win over any feature idea)

- Without pressing the button the room is a tiny closet with a small cat bed, so small all you can
  do is step in (and out).
- Press the button and then open the door: a whole herd of Sadies (a stampede, a lot of them) pours
  out, all meowing and carrying on, by various paths through the clubhouse, and out of the front door.
- Once the front door closes they vanish, with a little puff, not a blink. (The first version was
  meant to be too fast to follow to the front door; the owner said slow it down: you may watch them go.)
- The closet looks piled to the ceiling with Sadies when the door opens; they come out like an
  avalanche, with a caterwaul of meows at every pitch and a rush of noise.
- If the door is open when the button's pressed, it closes and straight away opens again with the
  avalanche.
- They can't move the player around: they're only pictures, they never push or block you.
