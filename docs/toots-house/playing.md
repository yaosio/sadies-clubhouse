# Toots' House: playing

## Outside
- Sky-blue siding, a red shingle roof, a red front door with a dog flap, shutters with bones cut out,
  WELCOME EVERYBODY on the mat, paw-print stepping stones, tennis balls in the gutter and the grass, a
  round attic window drawn as a tennis ball, her mailbox and sign, a fire hydrant on the lawn and a
  GUESTS doghouse that's a tiny copy of the house.
- Toots is always in her upstairs window, bigger than life (about 3 cm a dot) so you can see her face
  from the square. One paw on the sill: her right one (on your left as you look).
- Over 22 m away she watches the town. Closer, she spots you: ears up, eyes following you, the odd
  head tilt, and her roo (the first time you come near, then at most every 25 to 45 s). Within 7 m
  she's thrilled (her paw bounces), then drops out of sight, off downstairs; walk away and she's back.
  Those numbers are Claude's, changeable.

## Inside
Bigger inside than out (16 m by 12 m, two floors).
- Downstairs: the living room. The whole back wall is a big window onto the backyard (fence, tree,
  flowers, birdbath). A couch faces it. A fireplace with a picture of Toots over it, and on the right
  wall, framed pictures of her friends Sadie, Marbles and Chooter, each with a gold name plate. Her
  food and water bowls, a basket of tennis balls, a lamp, EVERYBODY IS WELCOME HERE over the door.
- The stairs run up the left wall.
- Upstairs: her window seat at the front window (the one you see her in from outside), her round red
  bed with a ball in it, and a little bed with Sadie asleep in it (Zs and all), a dresser and a rug.

## Fetch
- Look at the ball (or the basket) and PICK UP / TAKE it; look anywhere and THROW it. It bounces off
  walls, lands on things, rolls down the stairs.
- Toots runs after it, picks it up and brings it back to your feet. She follows you about between
  throws, up and down the stairs too, and hops over anything in her way. PAT TOOTS when she's close.
- She walks with a little hop at the front, the way three-legged dogs do.
- Now and then (every third fetch, and sometimes when patted) she roos.
- Nothing is saved.

## Her sounds
Two cuts of her real roo from the owner's window video (the voice at its very end is cut out), played
at slightly different speeds so they're never quite the same. At most one every 5 s, soft, fading
with distance, on the voices bus. Outside they belong to the house, so you hear her from the square.
