# Toots' House: history

How it was asked for. Never needed for a task; only read before undoing a choice.

- 2026-10-11: the owner asked for a house for Toots, their dog: a regular two-story house owned by a
  dog, beside the paint shop, her missing front left leg in every picture, her watching from the
  window, her real sounds from their videos.
- Made-up sounds were tried first and didn't sound like her, so her real roo is used (the voice at
  the end of the clip cut out).
- The owner asked to go inside: a big, really nice interior, two floors, fetch with a tennis ball,
  pictures of Sadie, Marbles and Chooter, beds for Toots and Sadie upstairs, with Sadie asleep.
- After playing it, the owner asked for: the glitches fixed, Sadie watching a bird TV instead of
  sleeping, more upstairs (another couch and other things), her own music with made-up barks, Toots
  happy instead of frowning, sharper pictures, and only one Toots (not at the window and the door at
  once).
- Then: the town all round out of every window (four snapshots), the fence joined to the house, a
  stutter at her door fixed (her pictures drawn a few at a time), and her roo only as her door opens
  (not from the window over and over), with every pat a roo once the last one's finished.
- Then a painted-on window on the back of the house outside, and a fix for her picture poking
  through the downstairs ceiling for a moment when you walked in before she'd left her window.
- Then her window picture cut off at the seat as she drops out of sight (it used to slide on down
  below the seat, and showed for a few frames).
- Then, if you beat her to the door, she comes bounding down the stairs to meet you (she used to just
  pop in a moment after the door opened).
