# Marbles' Cut & Curl: history

How it was asked for. Never needed for a task; only read before undoing a choice.

- The owner asked for it on 2026-10-03: a barbershop run by Marbles, a mischievous standard-issue
  tabby, with hairdos, taildos and outfits for Sadie, none saved. A music outfit with a short segment
  where she sings (all meows), a construction outfit where she builds a cat tower that falls over, a
  ball gown where she dances, and a few surprises. Hairdos and taildos have no segments.
- A 2D sketch with the sounds was made first (the owner heard them there). The owner liked the sounds
  and outfits, said Marbles doesn't steal anything from Sadie, and that the real shop is 3D.
