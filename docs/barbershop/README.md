# Marbles' Cut & Curl

The third building outside Sadie's front gate (`src/activities/barbershop/`), on the plot next to
Clyde's House (`lot` 2). A barbershop run by Marbles, a mischievous brown tabby. Sadie sits in the
barber chair and you give her a hairdo, a taildo and an outfit; some outfits come with a little show
on the stage. Nothing is saved. Everything outside the gate belongs to somebody else (the owner's
rule), so the shop is Marbles', and Sadie is her customer.

## Design pillars (these win over any feature idea)

- **Sadie is the toy.** You dress her and see her at once, in the mirror's chair, and nothing can go
  wrong or be lost: leave the shop and she's plain Sadie again.
- **Choosing is walking up to things.** Hair on wig heads, tails on ribbons, outfits on a rail, each
  one pressed with USE. No menu.
- **Outfits have shows, hairdos and taildos don't.** A show is about eight seconds, started by the
  rope by the stage, and can be walked away from: nothing holds you.
- **Marbles never takes anything from Sadie.** She's a trickster in what she picks and where she
  pops up, not a thief.
- **Kind to the ears** (the owner has misophonia, and dislikes only droning and ticking). Sounds
  play once, soft, when something happens. The music is a melody on the music line (never a drone
  or a steady beat): the shop's own tune while you're inside, and a short song with each show.
- **You can see what's pickable.** Bobbing arrows over every wig head, ribbon, hanger and Marbles
  (the rope's only when the outfit has a show), pink on what Sadie has on; and each outfit hangs as
  the garment it is.
