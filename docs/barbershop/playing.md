# Playing Marbles' Cut & Curl

Who's in the shop, what's in it, what Sadie can wear and what each show does. Read before changing
what the player meets. How it's built: `how-built.md`.

## The shop from outside
A cream shop with pink stripes and a black and white tiled bottom, a red and white awning, a sign
(MARBLES' CUT & CURL), two windows with mirrors and a cat in them, a barber pole turning by the door,
and a giant pair of scissors on the roof, opening and closing slowly. In through the red door.

## Inside
A mint room with a checkered floor. In the middle of the back wall a big mirror, and in front of it
the barber chair with Sadie in it. On the left wall a shelf of wig heads (HAIR), further along a rack
of ribbons (TAILS). Across the front right a clothes rail (OUTFITS). In the back right a stage with a
red curtain; the rope at its front left corner starts the show. Marbles stands by her counter in the
back left, winking now and then. Press USE on a wig head, ribbon or hanger to put it on Sadie. A yellow arrow bobs over each of
them (pink over what Sadie has on), over Marbles, and over the rope when the outfit has a show; each
outfit hangs as the garment it is. Marbles says PICK ANYTHING YOU LIKE! when you come in.

## Marbles
A brown tabby with an M on her forehead and a pair of scissors in her paw. Press USE on her and she
picks a hairdo, a taildo and an outfit herself (never the plain ones) and says something smug. She
comments now and then when you choose, and pops up in two shows.

## What Sadie can wear
- **Hairdos** (6, no shows): just Sadie, pompadour, pink mohawk, big beehive, pigtails, big perm.
  Hats sit on top of the hair.
- **Taildos** (6, no shows): plain, poodle pom, big red bow, candy stripe, lion tuft, glitter (a new
  sparkle pattern about once a second).
- **Outfits** (8): none, and seven with shows (below).

## The shows
Pulling the rope with a show outfit on: the curtain opens, Sadie hops up, the show runs, she hops
back. With no show outfit on, Marbles says there isn't one.
- **Pop star** (sing): a spotlight and a microphone; eight soft meows to a tune, notes floating up.
- **Hard hat + vest** (build): Sadie builds a cat tower one block at a time. It wobbles, and falls
  over in five soft thumps ("FWUMP.").
- **Ball gown** (dance): a music box waltz, Sadie sways and twirls, sparkles circle her.
- **Magician**: a cape goes over Sadie, poof, she's gone, and Marbles pops out of the top hat.
- **Superhero**: Sadie leaps into the air, flies across the stage and lands in a cardboard box
  ("IF I FITS...").
- **Detective**: she sniffs along the stage with a magnifying glass and finds a clue. Marbles is
  behind it holding her own tuna can ("WHAT TUNA?").
- **Pajamas**: she curls up and falls asleep, Zs floating up.

## Sounds and music
Sounds play once and stop: a tiny snip for every pick, the meow song, the tower's five thumps, a
poof, a whoosh and a thud, an a-ha chirp. Voices (Sadie) are on the VOICES volume.

Music (on the MUSIC volume, only heard in the shop, the clubhouse's own theme makes way for it): a
soft tune of about forty seconds, played round and round while you're inside. Each show has its own
song instead, starting as Sadie reaches the stage: a backing for the meows (pop star), a whistled
work tune that falls apart with the tower (hard hat), a music box waltz (ball gown), harp runs and a
ta-da (magician), a fanfare (superhero), tiptoeing bass and a sly tune (detective), a lullaby
(pajamas). The shop's tune comes back a moment after a song ends.

## What's saved
Nothing. Leave the shop (or reload) and Sadie is plain Sadie.
