# Lily's House: playing

## Outside
- A peach cottage on the hydrant's top ledge: pink scalloped shingles, orange trim, two round windows,
  a big bow on the gable (pink or orange, picked each time the game starts, the same as Lily's), a
  rubber-chicken weathervane, a trampoline and a HI! HI! HI! mat. Lily bounces in the left round
  window, and isn't there while you're inside (there's only one Lily).
- Step up onto the trampoline and you bounce, up and down, about 0.9 m, until you walk off; the
  chicken lying on it bounces with you.

## Inside
- The living room, like her real home: gray walls, white baseboards, beige tile, a vent, her round
  pink bed, a couch, a basket of rubber chickens, her bowls. Her friends' pictures on the right wall
  (Sadie, Chooter, Marbles, Toots), her own over the couch.
- Out of the two round windows: the real town (pictures of it taken once as the house is built, as in
  Toots' house).
- You hold her chicken. Looking down at her, HOLD UP THE CHICKEN (E, or HOLD UP on a phone): she
  crouches, leaps for it, prances round with it and brings it back. Looking ahead, THROW THE CHICKEN:
  it flies, bounces off the walls and floor and skids; she races after it, grabs it, and has the
  zoomies (darting about the room with it for a few seconds) before she brings it back. Dropped at
  your feet, you pick it up by walking up to it.
- Through the big GAME ROOM doorway: a tall room (taller than wide, like an arcade cabinet), beanbags,
  string lights, and the game filling the back wall.

## Lily's Big Climb
- PLAY LILY'S BIG CLIMB from anywhere in the game room: the view eases back to fit the wall. A, D or
  the arrows move, W and S climb, Space jumps, Esc steps back. On a phone: picture buttons along the
  bottom (left and right; up, down and jump), with STEP BACK moved to the top left so a thumb can't hit
  it by accident.
- One level, laid out like the old arcade's first stage. Lily starts by the bathtub at the bottom;
  Marbles sits up top throwing bath bubbles, hair dryers, vacuums and nail clippers (her first one each
  life drops straight down). They roll down the slanted girders, now and then down a ladder (much more
  often when Lily is somewhere below, as in the original), and into the tub, which can stir up a sud
  that wanders and climbs.
- Broken ladders can't be climbed. Lily can walk off an open end and fall; too far hurts. Two hammers
  hang in the air: jump up to one and for 9 seconds she bops things (300, a sud 500), but can't jump
  or climb. Jumping over a thing is 100. Three lives; a bonus of 5000 counts down 100 every 2 seconds.
- While a game's going, a quick arcade tune plays (music.js: square-wave notes that die away, a bass
  on the half bars, a breath between pieces; nothing droning or ticking). It stops as you step back.
- Reach her chicken on the top perch and she's won: the tune stops for a short fanfare, YOU GOT IT!, then you step back
  and walk about again. PLAY starts it again. Stepping back or pausing mid-game, it waits.
- Marbles throws every 1 to 2 seconds (Claude's numbers, changeable: the owner found it a touch easy
  at every 1.4 to 2.8).
