# Lily's House

The building on the top ledge of Dog City's giant hydrant (`src/activities/lily-house/`, `lot` 5,
Lily's ledge). Lily is the owner's third dog: a curly apricot doodle with a pink collar, a bow (pink or
orange) always on her left, a rubber chicken, and a love of jumping. A peach cottage outside where she
bounces in the round window and a trampoline you can bounce on; inside, her living room (hold up or
throw her chicken) and a
tall game room whose whole back wall is Lily's Big Climb, a one-level arcade climbing game.

## Design pillars (these win over any feature idea)

- **She jumps.** Everything she does is bouncy: in her window, for her chicken, up the girders.
- **Her bow is always on her left.** Every picture of her, in the room and in the game, has it there.
  Her two side views are drawn separately, never flipped (flipping would move the bow). Her tongue
  stays in.
- **The game plays like the old arcade climber, with our own pictures.** Its first stage's layout:
  slanted girders, full and broken ladders, two hammers, things rolling down, a tub that stirs up
  suds. No ape, barrels or plumber: Marbles throws bath things, Lily climbs for her chicken.
- **Music only at the game.** A bright arcade tune while you play and a short fanfare when you win;
  the rest of her house is quiet (nothing ticking or droning: `docs/clubhouse/RULEBOOK.md` section 4).
