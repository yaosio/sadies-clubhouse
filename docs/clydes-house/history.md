# Clyde's House: history

How it was asked for and what changed since. Never needed for a task; only read before undoing a
choice.

- The owner asked Claude on 2026-09-30 to build a house of its own just outside the gate, with a
  little path to it, and gave no other direction: its own character, its own house, and at least one
  activity inside that's interesting and fun, not generic.
- The character was first called Claude; the owner renamed it Clyde (same little spark, same
  house).
- The owner asked for the Weather Machine on 2026-09-30: Clyde's, so it stands beside his house,
  and outdoors, since it changes the weather for the whole world.
- Since 2026-10-02 the weather is the world's, not Clyde's (see `weather-machine.md`).
