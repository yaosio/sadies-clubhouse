# Dropper World's teasing numbers

Chooter snatching Sadie's hay and playing keep-away (`core/friends/chooter.js`). Read before
changing the teasing. Why he does it: `docs/dropper-world/characters/chooter.md`. Units:
`world-solver.md`.

Fed up after about 75 s of playing beside her (sooner if she's unimpressed with his ball: each "…"
counts for about 19 s), he snatches the hay she's going for if it's within 3.9 blocks of the pile,
leaping up for it.

Keep-away: when she comes within 3 blocks he picks a way to run and keeps going that way (4 blocks
at a time, 3.1 blocks/s) until she's more than 4 blocks behind, then bounces facing her. Backed into
a wall, he's caught: he turns to her with the hay. Drops it after 20 s.

Sadie runs after hay that's being carried, and eats it once it's within her reach.
