# The aquarium room

The room as the player sees it: the tank, Sadie, tapping the glass and the cabinet. Read before
changing the room. The sea is in `ocean.md`.

## The room and the tank

A room with a big fish tank along the back wall (what's in the room and the tank is listed under
`room.js` in `how-built.md`). Sadie swims in it in a little diving suit (a fishbowl helmet, an
orange air tank, yellow flippers) among the fish, on a lazy loop. A cardboard sign taped to the
glass says DON'T TAP ON THE GLASS!!.

## Tapping the glass

Stand at the glass and look at it, and the prompt shows: E, or the TAP GLASS button on a phone.
There are three spots along the glass to tap from, so wherever you stand at it you can tap it.
Tapping it takes you to the ocean (`ocean.md`).

## The OCEAN FINDS cabinet

Six spots, each a question mark until you've found its thing at sea; then the find sits there.

The room makes no sound; the test version's SOUND TEST box is in the pillars (`README.md`).
