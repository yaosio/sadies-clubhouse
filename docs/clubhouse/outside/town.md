# Levels, and growing into a town

How the outside will grow. Read before building anything outside that changes the ground or its
size.

The clubhouse is meant to sit on a hill in the middle of a town, one space you walk round, with
bridges, sewers and paths going over and under each other.
- Walking on more than one level already works outside (`floor(x, z, y)`: the ground and every
  `surface`, standing on whichever is nearest your feet; a step is at most half a metre).
- What's solid has a height, plots and spots have a height, and a sewer or a cave can be a place of
  its own behind a doorway.
- **Don't build anything new that assumes the outside is flat.**
- What a big town still needs is in `docs/clubhouse/decisions/known-limits.md`: the outside built
  whole, every house built at the start, the scenery's fixed size, one outside shared by every
  building, and putting away counted in doors.
