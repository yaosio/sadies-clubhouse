# TypeFitter Deluxe 3.1

The clubhouse's second activity (`src/activities/typefitter/`), built 2026-09-28, on the computer in
its room (its door is the third one up the stairs). A 1993 program by someone who loved what their
text engine could do and spent ten minutes on the box: every button changes the text, Sadie (a
flat, badly scanned picture traced from her real photo) gushes about it, FIT IT! never quite fits,
and you win anyway. It's a joke on the long fight to make Dropper World's dashboard text fit.

## Design pillars (the owner's rules; these win over any feature idea)

- The text can never fit its box. It's obviously broken on purpose, never by accident.
- The player always wins anyway.
- It shows off 90s text tricks (fresh after DOS).
- No puzzle, nothing for the game or the player to keep track of.
- Nothing carried over from Dropper World's mechanics.
- Sadie is a flat, static picture traced from her real photo (dithered, few colors, badly
  compressed, no outline round her) with a speech bubble of made-up facts.

## Its pages

- `playing.md`: how a round goes, the buttons and keys, and Sadie in TypeFitter; read before
  changing what the player does or what Sadie says.
- `numbers.md`: the meter, the box, the fit score, the pacing and what the tests expect; read
  before changing how quickly it fills or how badly the text misses.
- `look.md`: no windows, the text's tricks, Sadie's scanned picture, its box; read before changing
  how anything looks.
- `how-built.md`: which file does what, and its checks; read before changing the code.
- `parked.md`: parked ideas (none yet).
